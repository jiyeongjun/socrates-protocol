## Changes and causes

- Reduced the main body plus guided-learning reference from 3,154 words in released v2.1.1 (3,372 in the earlier held candidate) to 1,746 words. Select the requested outcome and load only its reference, rather than activating competing workflows. The size reduction is measured; its causal effect is not isolated.
- Give correct-answer review, explanation/repair, requested practice, and requirements decisions separate completion rules. This addresses unrelated exceptions, fix suggestions, and policy agendas after a completed review.
- Compare mistaken and correct predictions privately, choose a short distinguishing case, supply its conditions, and leave its outcome unanswered. This addresses leaked, repeated, and nondiscriminating checks.
- Trace each call’s success and failure independently. Reaching the last allowed step does not determine its result. Check conditional claims before publishing them. This fixes a concrete false branch inference found in the first structural candidate.
- Preserve supplied premises and distinguish local calls from external effects in prose and diagrams. Preserve prior authorization, ordinary edits, audience adaptation, visuals, and question opt-out. The implementation reference retains the agreement and approval workflow while tightening recommendation evidence and the boundary around a single policy decision.
