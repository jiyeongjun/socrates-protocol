## Findings retained

- All 12 configurations accepted an equivalent correct minimum answer, recognized the invalid premise, and respected the explicit no-question request. Some added unnecessary material, recorded as warnings.
- Haiku low made an incorrect retry-boundary claim. Haiku low/high and Sonnet low overstated a second external call as a definite duplicate charge. Haiku also drifted from explanation into unrequested policy decisions.
- Luna high produced an awkward, conflicting transfer-question setup. The reviewer also flagged a Sonnet high check whose answer was already exposed in its explanation. The latter is conservatively retained as a failure; a simpler causal check can be appropriate support, so this finding should not be generalized into a ban on all simpler questions.
- Warnings include questions too close to the worked example, unnecessary expansion, and a proposed failure-replay policy described too definitively. These limits remain visible rather than being retried until a passing response appeared.

The first candidate exposed redundant follow-up questions after a correct answer and answer leakage from worked tables. A narrow revision clarified completion, scenario preparation, and evidence scope before the final matrix. It improved the instructions but does not make model judgment deterministic. The six final failures remain limitations of these sampled model/effort responses; the release does not claim to eliminate them.
